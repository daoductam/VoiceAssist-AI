## Motivation
When a user sets a one-time alarm (`repeatDays: []`), after it rings and is dismissed, the alarm toggle switch remains ON (`isActive = true`), and the home screen hero card mistakenly rolls the target time 24 hours forward, displaying "Còn 23 giờ 59 phút nữa sẽ lặp lại báo thức kế tiếp".

## Success Criteria
| # | Criterion | How to Measure |
|---|-----------|---------------|
| 1 | One-time alarm dismiss deactivates alarm | When user dismisses an alarm with `repeatDays.length === 0`, `isActive` becomes `false` in SQLite DB and Zustand store |
| 2 | Native Android dismiss / timeout deactivates one-time alarm | When Android native playback stops, the one-time alarm is deactivated |
| 3 | Home screen countdown accuracy | Expired or ringing one-time alarms do not roll over to tomorrow (+24h) in `getNextAlarmTrigger`; only recurring alarms roll forward |
| 4 | No regression for recurring alarms | Alarms with `repeatDays.length > 0` remain active and roll forward to their next scheduled day of the week |

## Out of Scope
